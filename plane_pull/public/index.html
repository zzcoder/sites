How to Join and Donate

Use the team page to join Healthy Hikers, make your donation, and request any employer match.

Open Team Page
  1. Open the Healthy Hikers team page. Go to impact.specialolympicsva.org/team/832142.
  2. Create an account if needed. Please sign up with your real name as it appears on your driver’s license.
  3. Join Team Healthy Hikers. Even if you are not pulling, please join the team so you can serve as a backup. The team may have more than 25 people.
  4. Return to the team page and donate. Click the Donate button on the team page. Only people on the puller list are required to donate $100; donations from backups and supporters are optional.
  5. Request employer matching if available. If your employer matches donations, submit the match request right away because processing can take a month or longer.
  6. Check the admin-fee option. The donation page selects an admin-fee contribution by default, which changes the total to $111.08. You may uncheck that box and pay only $100.
