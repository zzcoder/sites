中文版

免责声明

鉴于本群组活动具有自愿性、非营利性及一定风险性，为明确各方权利义务，特作如下声明：

第一条（行为能力与风险自负）

本群全体成员均确认自身为具有完全民事行为能力的成年人，并已充分了解参与本群组织或相关活动可能存在的风险。成员自愿参与即视为接受并自行承担因此产生的一切风险及法律后果。

第二条（免责范围）

本人及本人的亲属、朋友或其他任何关联方，均不得就参与本群活动所生之任何损害、损失或纠纷，向群主或其他群成员提出索赔、赔偿或其他任何形式的主张；群内其他成员对此亦不负任何连带责任。

第三条（群外行为）

凡发生于本群组织活动范围之外的个人行为及其后果，均与群主及其他任何群成员无关，相关责任由行为人自行承担。

第四条（搭乘相关权利放弃）

接受搭乘服务的人员，自愿放弃现在及将来基于该次搭乘，向车辆提供者及驾驶员提起诉讼、追偿或主张赔偿的一切权利，无论驾驶员对相关事件是否存在过错。搭乘人员就该事件向第三方进行追偿时，驾驶员不负有提供协助或便利的义务。

第五条（同意与确认）

凡加入本群者，均视为已完整阅读、理解本声明全部条款，并明确表示无任何异议，自愿接受本声明之约束。

第六条（效力期间）

本声明自成员加入本群之时起生效，长期持续有效，不受时间限制。

第七条（适用法律与责任限制）

本声明适用相关活动发生地之法律，可能包括华盛顿特区、马里兰州、弗吉尼亚州、西弗吉尼亚州或宾夕法尼亚州，视具体活动地点而定。各方理解并认可，不同司法管辖区对本声明中风险自负及责任豁免条款的可执行性可能存在差异；其中，弗吉尼亚州法律一般不承认涉及人身伤害的事先责任豁免条款。若本声明中任一条款依据特定司法管辖区法律被认定为无效或不可执行，不影响该条款在其他司法管辖区的效力，亦不影响本声明在该司法管辖区内其他可执行部分的效力。无论适用何地法律，本声明任何条款均不得免除驾驶员或相关人员因故意行为或重大过失造成他人人身伤害、死亡或财产损失所应承担的、依法不可放弃的法律责任。

English Version

Liability Waiver and Release

Given that activities organized through this group are voluntary, non-commercial, and may involve inherent risk, the following terms are set forth to clarify the rights and responsibilities of all parties:

Article 1 (Capacity and Assumption of Risk)

All members of this group confirm that they are adults with full legal capacity and have a complete understanding of the risks that may be involved in participating in activities organized by or associated with this group. Voluntary participation constitutes acceptance of, and agreement to assume, any and all risks and legal consequences arising therefrom.

Article 2 (Scope of Release)

Neither the participant nor their family members, friends, or any other related parties may bring any claim, demand, or request for compensation of any kind against the group organizer or any other group member arising from participation in group activities. Other group members likewise assume no joint or several liability.

Article 3 (Conduct Outside Group Activities)

Any personal conduct occurring outside the scope of activities organized by this group, and any consequences thereof, are unrelated to the group organizer or any other group member; responsibility rests solely with the individual concerned.

Article 4 (Waiver of Claims Related to Ridesharing)

Any individual accepting a ride arranged through this group voluntarily waives, now and in the future, any right to sue or seek recovery against the vehicle provider or driver arising from that ride, regardless of whether the driver was at fault. Should the rider seek recovery from a third party in connection with such an incident, the driver shall have no obligation to provide assistance or facilitation of any kind.

Article 5 (Acknowledgment and Agreement)

By joining this group, an individual is deemed to have fully read and understood all provisions of this waiver, to have no objection to its terms, and to voluntarily agree to be bound by it.

Article 6 (Duration)

This waiver takes effect upon a member joining the group and shall remain in effect indefinitely, without limitation as to time.

Article 7 (Governing Law and Limitation of Liability)

This waiver shall be governed by the law of the jurisdiction in which the relevant activity takes place, which may include the District of Columbia, the State of Maryland, the Commonwealth of Virginia, the State of West Virginia, or the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, as applicable. The parties acknowledge that the enforceability of the risk-assumption and liability-release provisions in this waiver may vary by jurisdiction; in particular, Virginia law generally does not recognize pre-injury releases of liability for personal injury. If any provision of this waiver is held unenforceable under the law of a particular jurisdiction, that holding shall not affect the enforceability of that provision in any other jurisdiction, nor the enforceability of the remaining provisions of this waiver in that jurisdiction. Regardless of which jurisdiction’s law applies, no provision of this waiver shall be construed to release any driver or related party from liability that cannot, as a matter of law, be waived — including liability for personal injury, death, or property damage caused by intentional misconduct or gross negligence.
